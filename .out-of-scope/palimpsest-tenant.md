# The Palimpsest Tenant

This project will not grow a Tenant for the Platform's revised meaning — the
proposed "Palimpsest", which would have shown current wording printed normally
with the older, replaced wording bleeding faintly through underneath, like a
manuscript scraped clean and written over again.

## Why this is out of scope

It was a genuinely appealing idea, and it was the owner's own. It is rejected on
cost and overlap rather than on merit.

A Tenant is not a cheap unit. Chartering one commits the project to a fresh
visual design, its own content rules and schema, a planning pass (the original
write-up deliberately left the structure unsettled and said not to build
straight from it), and — decisively — a body of hand-written entries. The Midden,
the closest comparable Tenant, runs to roughly sixty content files. The
Palimpsest's material is the project's own renamed terms and overruled
decisions, which arrive slowly; the curation burden would be permanent while the
haul stayed thin.

The overlap is the other half. The Midden already catalogues what the Platform
discarded, and the proposed second half of the Palimpsest — reverted code and
abandoned approaches — is largely what the Midden's dig reports already narrate.
Only the words-and-decisions half was distinct, and that material is already
recorded where it belongs: superseded decisions live in the ADRs as amendments
and superseding records, and retired vocabulary lives in the glossaries'
revision history.

## The loose end this leaves, and how it was closed

The Midden's inclusion bar routed Gate-B failures — candidates that were
renamed or superseded rather than discarded — to "Palimpsest territory", a home
that now will never exist. That forward pointer has been removed from
`layers/midden/CONTEXT.md` and from the `midden-survey` Skill. Those candidates
are now simply out of the Midden's scope, recorded as Gate-B exclusions in the
survey report so they are never silently dropped.
