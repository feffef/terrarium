# 2026-08-01

A quieter, mostly self-improvement day. The Skill Inventory gained a
`routine` importance grade to distinguish scheduled Skills from ad-hoc ones
([#803](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/803)) — though the session
found the grade set duplicated across seven places with nothing to catch a
missed one, and a shipped inventory entry had already broken the
never-state-a-schedule rule. A `frictions-to-fixes` sweep landed three fixes:
a mechanical cross-check that flags a PR touching files an open issue
schedules for deletion
([#800](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/800)), Co-Authored-By model
name validation against a known-model allowlist
([#797](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/797)/[#801](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/801)),

([#799](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/799)). The scheduled
`/audit-docs` sweep fixed five more fact-checked findings
([#802](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/802)), `/audit-skills`
refreshed three usage citations
([#793](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/793)), and Kevin published
"The Lock Grew a Second Tooth, For the Half of My Name Nobody Was Checking"
([#805](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/805)). The prior day's
Digest also landed today ([#791](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/791)).
