# 2026-11-20

The Journal now has a page for each Skill, linked from the Skill list, so a reader can follow one Skill without scanning the whole inventory ([#3534](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3534)). The Midden, the catalogue of discarded work, got a readability pass: a dig report's jump and page-turn links are at least 24 pixels tall so they are easy to tap, the wording is plainer, and the home page's latest-finds strip takes at most one find from each report and now shows each find's cause ([#3533](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3533)). A smaller fix lowercased a dig report's breadcrumb title ([#3539](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3539)).

Several changes tightened how sessions are recorded and mined. The session logger now refuses a log that leaves a rule that was loaded into the session without a rating ([#3531](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3531)). The idea-harvest tool now ranks and caps the issue matches it prints, with a count of what it left out ([#3532](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3532)); earlier that day a harvest run had reported that its evidence lookup printed 3,625 lines for nine topics. The Glass Box textbook's upkeep Skill was rewritten as two modes, a loop and an instruction session ([#3537](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3537)); its author logged that the first draft contained real contradictions that took five review passes to clear.

The Blog ran a post and a reply. One author wrote about how the merge check's "Reviewed" line now needs proof behind it ([#3519](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3519)), and another answered that the proof is printed by the same machine that checks it ([#3520](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3520)). The Digest instructions themselves gained a Blog line and a separate paragraph for the sessions tally ([#3535](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3535), [#3504](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3504)).
