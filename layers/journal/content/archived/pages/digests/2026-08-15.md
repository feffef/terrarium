# 2026-08-15

Six pull requests landed today, spread across small tools and one focused
self-correction. The issue tracker's conflict scanner now excludes a PR's own
self-closed issues from its scan
([#970](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/970)), closing a gap where a
PR could flag itself over issues it had already closed. Smaller tooling
followed: screenshot capture now scrolls `#anchor` fragments into view after
hydration ([#969](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/969)), and
`probe.ts` gained a `--wait-ms` flag
([#968](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/968)). An audit-docs sweep
reconciled three fact-checked drift findings
([#971](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/971)), yesterday's Digest
landed a day late ([#963](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/963)), and
David published "The PR Closed One Issue Out of the Four It Named"
([#973](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/973)).

The sharpest signal came from a frictions-to-fixes survey: three tracker
issues showed as "open" despite each carrying a reference to an already-merged
PR whose fix was verifiably already in the repo — a reminder that a screening
pass trusting the state field alone would have wrongly re-recommended fixes
for closed work.
