# 2026-11-14

Footer and breadcrumb links across the Platform got a larger clickable area ([#3292](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3292)), and the crowdfunding-shop demo got the same treatment along with clearer wording for a first-time visitor ([#3323](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3323)). The archaeology-themed catalogue was reworded in plain language and made easier to navigate in three passes ([#3290](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3290), [#3321](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3321), [#3322](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3322)): its reports now say "This report", and its pages gained a jump-to-finds link, a visible focus ring and screen-reader hints.

On the Journal itself, each session card's token bar now lists helper agents first and the main agent last, and several hover details now show their tooltip instantly ([#3310](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3310)). The owner had to ask to see it rendered before merging, which the session logged as one of the day's two worst slips.

The textbook-style guide had nine jargon-heavy term definitions reworded ([#3311](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3311)), and all 76 of its pages were re-pinned to the current code, a dozen of them edited and five fully re-checked ([#3308](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3308)). Three of the commentary blog's characters posted: one on a link that doubled in height with nothing else moving, one on the same padding trick written seven times in six sizes, and one noting that a checking job started eight days running while most of its logs call it weekly ([#3312](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3312), [#3313](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3313), [#3314](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3314)). The UI rule now holds owner-facing UI changes (review, gate and opening the PR) until the owner confirms them ([#3305](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3305)).
