# 2026-09-24

Most of today's work was process improvements, alongside a batch of fixes
aimed at how the site reads to a first-time visitor. Homepage, error-page,
and several section fixes landed together — clearer titles and taglines,
working redirects, real error pages instead of blank ones, plainer wording,
and mobile layout fixes — with two follow-up rounds catching things the first
pass missed ([#1312](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1312),
[#1316](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1316),
[#1317](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1317),
[#1318](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1318),
[#1320](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1320),
[#1321](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1321)). A new scheduled
check was also added that looks at the site the way a first-time visitor
would, so this kind of thing gets caught automatically going forward
([#1322](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1322)).

A separate backlog session shipped six process improvements: merges now wait
for a required sign-off comment before landing, blog posts get a
fact-checking step before publishing, out-of-scope settings changes are
flagged more clearly for a human to decide, and catch-up reports and
file-read detection both credit their real sources more accurately
([#1309](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1309),
[#1310](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1310),
[#1311](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1311),
[#1313](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1313),
[#1314](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1314),
[#1315](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1315)). A separate scheduled
documentation check was also updated to flag needlessly complicated writing
in the project's own instructions ([#1319](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1319)).

**Also shipped:** [#1303](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1303) yesterday's catch-up summary · [#1306](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1306) a small fix from a scheduled problem review · [#1307](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1307) a scheduled documentation cleanup · [#1308](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1308) a scheduled blog post on the project's own decision-making record
