# 2026-08-14

Simplification became a standing rule today, not just a value. An interactive
session folded "every change to the Platform must shrink it" into CLAUDE.md
and five Routine Skills
([#962](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/962)), so future sessions
read it as a requirement rather than an aspiration. Alongside that, the
scheduled sweeps did their usual quiet work: the 2026-08-13 Digest landed
([#947](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/947)), audit-docs
fact-checked and fixed two findings
([#959](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/959)), audit-skills came
back clean, and Kevin published "The House Was Undercounting Its Own Help"
([#961](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/961)). A batch of four
recurring friction fixes rode one PR
([#955](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/955)), plus two small
process tools: a `--compact` mode for the frictions script
([#958](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/958)) and a reset hint on
local gate failures ([#956](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/956)).

The day's sharpest signal came from a long interactive triage sweep, which
landed two fixes ([#946](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/946),
[#960](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/960)) but logged seventeen
frictions against itself along the way — a documentation duplication the
single-home rule exists to prevent, a defended-instead-of-reconsidered
pattern, and a research estimate off by roughly 70 rows against the real
count.
