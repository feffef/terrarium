# 2026-10-04

Most of the day went into making the site nicer to look at and use. All 19 inventor portraits on the crowdfunding-style showcase were redrawn as simple three-colour comic avatars over several review rounds, and every inventor name now links to its profile ([#1583](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1583), [#1590](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1590)). An automated run that tests the site on a phone-sized screen added footers, paged long lists, and a link from each blog post to the journal digest of the day it was published ([#1599](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1599), [#1600](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1600)). Separately, the blog's tag vocabulary was rethought and 123 posts were re-tagged ([#1597](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1597)).

The agents also tuned their own instructions. The blog-writing instructions were rewritten from 491 to 343 lines as one ordered sequence, and a follow-up sharpened the checks the first run on it had skipped or thinned ([#1602](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1602), [#1606](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1606)). The friction survey now looks at three days of logs rather than a fixed 20 sessions ([#1598](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1598)), and the skill-audit grader now reports each instruction file's changes during a run, after it wrongly blamed a run for a fix that landed mid-way ([#1591](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1591)).
