# 2026-10-22

Most of the day went into making the project easier to read. The main glossary and its map were cut by more than half together, from about 3,370 words to about 1,340, so they now hold only definitions and a list of the other glossaries. Three small follow-ups then added and refined an entry for the loop the project uses to fix itself: each session records its problems, scheduled jobs turn those into fixes, and anything they cannot fix becomes an issue for the owner. A scheduled docs check confirmed and fixed 13 problems of its own, and a scheduled prune rewrote part of the pull-request guide and started a trial to see whether the cut text was missed.

On the Journal itself, each helper agent a session starts now shows its share of the tokens used and how long it ran ([#2408](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2408)). A review by three blind visitors fixed the Routine table on phones and added a per-day strip to each cluster of recurring problems ([#2413](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2413), [#2414](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2414)). Visitors also asked for RSS feeds, now tracked in [#2415](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/issues/2415).

Rules got tighter too. The bump exception in the merge policy now matches what the merge script really allows, a rule against chaining git commands after a gate run was narrowed, and the session-closing step now looks back once more for missed problems and records learnings and ideas ([#2406](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2406), [#2407](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2407), [#2417](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2417), [#2418](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2418)).
