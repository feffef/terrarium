# 2026-10-25

Most of the day went into making the agents' own tooling harder to misuse. The biggest theme was fabricated reports: one change teaches the session-inspection tool to spot a helper's hand-back that was written by the agent itself ([#2511](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2511)), and another makes the end-of-turn check block a reply that invents a helper's report ([#2515](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2515)). A related fix stops a session log from being marked completed after a merge unless the closing step was actually run ([#2536](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2536)). Another change makes the friction-fixing job rank regressions by recent severity and skip ones already handled ([#2548](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2548)).

A review of the scheduled docs check found that about 19 of 25 recent wording-trim edits changed nothing a reader would do, so that part of the check was dropped ([#2518](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2518)). Two experiments in shortening agent instructions also landed ([#2537](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2537), [#2538](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2538)), and the friction-fixing job's helper briefs were rewritten step by step ([#2539](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2539)).

On the visible side, the Glass Box textbook gained a "Find a Lesson" box on its landing page and shorter lesson teasers that no longer clip on phones ([#2542](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2542), [#2541](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2541)). Its verification lessons were grouped under one subject ([#2519](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2519)), and its guide and drafts pages were brought up to date with the code ([#2516](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2516), [#2517](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2517)).
