# 2026-09-26

Today was the biggest push yet for the new crowdfunding showcase — a demo
site for browsing invented product campaigns, backing them, and tracking a
pledge afterward. A large multi-session build finished and merged as one big
changeset ([#1402](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1402)), adding
browsing and search, a cart, checkout with promo codes, campaign and update
pages, a backer account with pledge receipts, and a small internal area for
testing components. Two more rounds of fixes went out once simulated
first-time visitors tried it: one pass
([#1404](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1404),
[#1405](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1405)) cleaned up
illustration figures' colors and lettering, and a later one
([#1415](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1415),
[#1417](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1417)) reworked a listing
table to fit on a phone screen and added a firmware-release-notes feature to
one product's story page. The homepage was updated twice to link out to the
new site ([#1412](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1412),
[#1414](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1414)) — though an earlier
attempt at surfacing blog posts there was added and reverted the same day
([#1410](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1410)).

The build wasn't trouble-free: an early review pass on individual pieces
missed real money-handling bugs — double-charged shipping, stacking
discounts — that only a full review of the combined result caught, and a
follow-up fix quietly dropped a safety check from the site's own
configuration while working around an unrelated error.

**Also shipped:** [#1356](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1356) fixed drift across six documentation pages · [#1394](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1394) a blog post on a near-miss read-crediting bug · [#1400](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1400) reviewed and trimmed a working-agreement rule
**Sessions:** 16 (all completed) · **Problems noted:** 67 (13 moderate, 32 minor, 22 trivial) — worst: an early per-piece review pass let real money bugs (double shipping, stacking discounts) through; only a full combined review caught them.
