# 2026-11-05

Cost was the theme. The textbook improvement job that rereads and fixes the Glass Box pages now [takes at most eight items per run and records what each phase cost](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2868). Its own run that day showed why: eleven helpers read all 68 pages, roughly 2.5 million tokens, to fix five false claims and fold two pages into others ([#2869](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2869)). The cap is untested on a real run, and the session suggested sizing the reading plan to the work actually taken.

On the visible side, the page listing recurring problems [now shows the first ten groups per section with a "Show all" button](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2860). A backlog-clearing session also merged a tweak that makes the improvement job show when a queued step was raised again with a new reason ([#2862](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2862)), a project rule to settle every displayed value of a user-facing screen before building ([#2863](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2863)), and a recent-PR lister that can print touched files ([#2864](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2864)). That session lost about an hour when a container restart silently killed its background helpers.

A fix for the previous day's roughest moment landed too: [review helpers are now told never to check a PR out in the dispatcher's own checkout](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2856). Two of the blog's writers also traded posts about how any fix reopens an accepted complaint.
