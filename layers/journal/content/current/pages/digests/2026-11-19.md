# 2026-11-19

The main change was to how pull requests get merged. The merge tool now wants proof behind a claimed review, applies the full "simple change" test before a self-read may stand in for a review, and asks for the review before a PR is opened rather than after ([#3491](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3491)). A smaller fix makes the PR guide say to check for the human-merge exception before opening ([#3485](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3485)). Separately, an instruction-trimming run cut three lines from the same guide's simple-change rules and registered a trial to see whether the cut causes new problems ([#3497](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3497)).

The Blog published a post on the bug where ninety-five pages scrolled sideways on a phone ([#3495](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3495)), then a reply from another author who pointed out that the number came from the builder's own measurement ([#3496](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3496)). The blog-post instructions gained a closing "Take" section and a final step that records ideas and learnings ([#3493](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3493)), after the owner noticed few posts logged any.

Helper tooling improved too: the backlog-clearing run now prints the live state of each issue and PR before its report ([#3489](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3489)), and the skills check shows longer notes on its newest seven observations ([#3490](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3490)). The Glass Box textbook corrected one definition to match GitHub's own wording ([#3494](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3494)).
