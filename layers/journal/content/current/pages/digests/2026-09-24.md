# 2026-09-24

Today's big story was standing up a brand-new self-checking routine: simulated
first-time visitors were sent through the live site, their complaints were
checked against the real pages, and every genuine problem was fixed — a
collapsing panel, confusing mobile navigation, a missing shared footer, broken
error pages, and rough layouts across several sections
([#1312](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1312),
[#1316](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1316),
[#1317](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1317),
[#1318](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1318)). Four of those fixes
had nearly gone out with no second pair of eyes on them at all until someone
asked; a proper check then caught real bugs, including one change that broke
site-wide navigation rules, so the routine now checks its own work before
merging anything itself. Once running daily on its own
([#1322](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1322)), its very first
automatic run found and fixed six more real problems plus added a small new
homepage feature showing recent activity across the site
([#1324](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1324),
[#1326](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1326)). Separately, someone
noticed that a piece of plain text containing an unquoted hash symbol can get
silently cut off wherever this project stores structured content, not only in
its internal logs — a quiet reminder that such truncation is a real risk, not
just a logging quirk.
