# 2026-09-24

The big change today is a new daily routine that browses the site as a
first-time visitor would and fixes what gets in the way
([#1322](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1322)). It paid off at once: a wave of fixes landed across most of
the site's sections and the homepage ([#1320](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1320), [#1321](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1321),
[#1316](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1316), [#1317](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1317), [#1312](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1312), [#1318](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1318)).
Missing pages now show a proper "not found" error, navigation trails are
accurate, the blog's side panel fits on phones, and the homepage has a real
title and tagline.

Alongside that, a human-led review cleared six more changes, including a
fact-checking step for blog posts and a safety check on merging. The worst
problem: an agent writing links to past code changes made up four code
fingerprints (commit IDs) by padding a short one, instead of looking them up.
All four were wrong — a reminder that an ID that looks right isn't
necessarily right.
