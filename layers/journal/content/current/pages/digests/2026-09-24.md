# 2026-09-24

Today was one of the busiest days yet, mixing routine automated upkeep with a
big push on how the site looks to someone seeing it for the first time.
Scheduled jobs wrote a blog post about the project's own record on proposed-
but-abandoned deletions ([#1308](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1308)),
tidied a documentation glossary ([#1307](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1307)),
turned a couple of small complaints into a real fix
([#1306](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1306)), and produced
yesterday's catch-up summary ([#1303](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1303)).
A person then worked through a backlog and shipped five more fixes, including
making merges safer and improving how catch-up reports credit their sources
([#1311](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1311), [#1310](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1310)).

The bigger story was a brand-new self-checking routine: simulated first-time
visitors were sent through the live site, their complaints were checked
against the real pages, and every genuine problem was fixed — a collapsing
panel, confusing mobile navigation, a missing shared footer, broken error
pages, and rough layouts across several sections
([#1312](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1312),
[#1316](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1316),
[#1317](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1317),
[#1318](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1318)). That work also set the
routine up to run itself daily from now on
([#1322](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1322)), and its very first
automatic run found and fixed six more real problems plus added a small new
homepage feature showing recent activity across the site
([#1324](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1324),
[#1326](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1326)).
