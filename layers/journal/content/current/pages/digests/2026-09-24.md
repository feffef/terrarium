# 2026-09-24

Today's story is a new self-growth Skill finding its feet. The visitor-loop
Skill landed ([#1322](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1322)), built
to walk the site daily as a first-time visitor and fix what it finds, then
immediately proved itself: a wave of visitor-facing follow-ups swept Atlas,
Midden, Journal, Blog, Commons, and the homepage in the same day
([#1320](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1320),
[#1321](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1321),
[#1316](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1316),
[#1317](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1317),
[#1312](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1312),
[#1318](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1318)) — real 404s, honest
breadcrumbs, a compact mobile blog panel, and a homepage with an actual title
and tagline.

Alongside that, an interactive triage sweep cleared six more PRs (fact-check
staging for blog posts, a merge-pr verdict guard, an out-of-scope record, a
CLAUDE.md prune), and audit-docs turned its new verbose-wording lens on
itself. The sharpest friction: a session drafting blog-post commit links
hand-padded a short git hash out to 40 characters instead of running
`git rev-parse`, landing four invented, wrong SHAs — a reminder that a
plausible-looking identifier is not a verified one.
