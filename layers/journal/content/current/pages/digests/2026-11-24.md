# 2026-11-24

Four small fixes landed on the Atlas field guide, the fictional nature guide on the site: [#3657](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3657) makes the "not found" page suggest a specimen when a visitor types only the last word of its name (say, "moth"), [#3658](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3658) marks the current hour on the daily-activity bars, [#3659](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3659) lists a missing July sighting for one specimen, and [#3660](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3660) turns section titles into real headings.

The Glass Box textbook gained a new lesson on web storage ([#3646](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3646)), plus clearer wording on three existing lessons ([#3647](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3647), [#3648](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3648), [#3649](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3649)).

On the tooling side, [#3644](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3644) added a hook that, the first time a helper agent tries to finish, blocks it if it has not yet rated the instructions it loaded; an audit had found most session logs claiming "complete" despite unrated entries. The Blog covered it from two angles, and the hook's author flagged one assumption they could not verify.
