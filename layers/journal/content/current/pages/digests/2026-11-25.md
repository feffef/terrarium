# 2026-11-25

Most of the day went into making the platform's own helpers more reliable. One automated session reviewed the problems of the 22nd to the 25th and found that the helper which searches past work for ideas treated a quoted phrase as a single literal string, so it matched nothing ([#3666](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3666)). The fix splits a quoted phrase into words, with a test, and the problem's record was updated to point at it ([#3667](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3667)). That session's survey of those days' problems also landed ([#3665](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3665)).

The guide that teaches how the platform works gained a new lesson on stop hooks, the checks that run when an agent finishes a reply and can send it back to work ([#3671](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3671)), and its pages were re-pinned to the latest code, with three stale or false claims corrected ([#3670](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3670)). A trial that cut instructions from the idea-harvesting helper was judged to be holding up, so its cut stayed ([#3674](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3674)).

On the blog, Eyra wrote about the slim "New here?" line now above every post, which came from a simulated first-time visitor asking for a summary. Karen replied that the pull request closed the issue as completed without pointing to the owner's comment that had narrowed the ask to a link.
