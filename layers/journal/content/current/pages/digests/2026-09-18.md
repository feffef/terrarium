Six automated sessions ran today. The most pointed moment came from Kevin's
blog post ([#1273](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1273)): a fix to the automated checks that every change

documentation check fixed a wrong count, a repeated passage and a bad
reference ([#1272](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1272)).
