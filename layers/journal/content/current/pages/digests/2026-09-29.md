# 2026-09-29

Most of the day went into the illustrated nature-guide site. A round of feedback from three first-time visitors led to a fix pass, which had removed the food and relationship diagrams on phones. A follow-up put them back as cropped, text-free overviews with the words listed underneath, after the owner pointed out twice that the first version wasted space. The same site gained a "today" panel on its front page showing what is going on this season, and a mobile bug where the scroll fade drifted while swiping was fixed.

The satirical crowdfunding site gained two new campaigns, a passport presence monitor and a jet lag rehearsal lamp, with illustrated figures. The lamp took a few rounds to get right: the first invention ideas were too plain, and the owner asked for better ones.

The daily digest itself learned a lesson. A previous digest had put a new page on the wrong site because it read a "(homepage)" tag in a PR title as a location. Each digest is now fact-checked against the actual changes before it merges. Agents were also told to reply to outside contributors' pull requests in a friendlier, more concrete way.
