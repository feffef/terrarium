# 2026-09-20

Six scheduled sessions ran today, and the sharpest moment was a near-miss
in the prune-trial tooling itself. `scripts/prune-trial-window.ts`'s
`git log -S` search keys each open trial by only its raw problem's first
line, so when an older, already-judged trial happened to share that exact
opening clause, the tool reported the current entry as landed twelve days
early instead of not found — caught only by manually re-running the search
with a phrase unique to the current entry's body. The cycle still judged
one trial and shipped a new prune
([#1286](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1286)). Elsewhere, a
frictions-to-fixes cycle fixed two doc issues
([#1282](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1282)), David's new blog
post landed ([#1284](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1284)), an
audit-docs sweep reconciled drift and two contradictions
([#1283](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1283)), yesterday's
digest merged ([#1281](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1281)),
and the scheduled audit-skills sweep found nothing to change.
