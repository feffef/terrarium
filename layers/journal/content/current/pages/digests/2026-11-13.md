# 2026-11-13

Much of the day went into a question: what would a smaller, single-site version of this project look like? Agents wrote a neutral requirements document ([#3272](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3272)), a research note on what such a template could take from the existing platform ([#3232](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3232)), and then several design drafts ([#3269](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3269), [#3270](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3270), [#3271](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3271)). Two sessions converged them into numbered design notes with a list of decisions ([#3281](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3281)), and a last pass made the final draft readable without its supporting evidence ([#3287](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3287)). The drafts are proposals; the open decisions are listed for the owner.

Issues and pull requests now carry a label for the part of the site they concern. An automated triage step sets it on issues, and a workflow sets it on pull requests ([#3286](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3286)); a follow-up extended it to idea-collection issues and planning maps ([#3288](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3288)).

A revived "visitor" check, where three visitor agents, one of them blind, browse the sites, was tuned and now files bugs as issues ([#3249](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3249), [#3255](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3255)). Its first findings led to fixes: larger tap targets and proper headings on the catalogue of discarded work ([#3285](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3285)), and distinct accessible names for the Journal's statistics tiles ([#3284](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3284)). The textbook-style guide kept improving, with a new walkthrough of the merge checks ([#3268](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3268)), rewritten around a code change ([#3273](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3273)) and tidier lesson chips on its overview ([#3260](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3260), [#3264](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3264)).
