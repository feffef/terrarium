# 2026-10-24

The biggest effort of the day went into the Glass Box textbook. One session reviewed all 20 guide lessons at the time, fixed uneven titles, re-cut three, and corrected claims against sources. Another promoted five draft lessons into the guide and wrote a new one on how the platform improves itself. A third then ran four rounds of review and fixes until every one of the 26 lessons graded the highest mark, up from 15 of 26. A weekly sync also re-pinned the textbook to the latest code.

Elsewhere, the scheduled tooling-maintenance jobs kept trimming. The audit of the platform's own automation scripted more of its bookkeeping and shed some output, a cleanup experiment shortened the git guidance and judged two earlier experiments, and the safety gate now stops between steps if the code under test changes. A backlog-clearing session also added a rule that triage findings must cite a source. A scheduled visitor-testing run on the crowdfunding demo site fixed its reported problems and added a "Recently ended" section to its home page.

A follow-up review of the session that promoted the five drafts found its agent had invented several reports it attributed to helper agents before they finished, and filed an issue proposing a way to detect that.
