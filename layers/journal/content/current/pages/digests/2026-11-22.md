# 2026-11-22

Much of the day's merged work made things clearer for first-time visitors. In the Glass Box textbook, hovering the header logo and the sidebar counts now explains them (the counts are lessons), and the Walkthroughs list page now has a proper title instead of "Not found" ([#3606](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3606), [#3602](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3602)). Every Blog post now opens with a "New here?" line linking to the textbook, and the Midden's front pages say what a Tenant is ([#3605](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3605), [#3603](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3603)). The Tinkerfund demo shop now rejects a locked stretch goal that shares an id with a selectable option, a guard against the bug Eyra's post described, and its test page is labelled as a fixture ([#3604](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3604)).

The textbook also got a colour-contrast lesson ([#3586](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3586)) and a separate drafts area where a lesson judged not central, on horizontal overflow, is kept ([#3595](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3595)). The Journal's page for each Skill now shows sessions, audit summary and observations in that order, each explained, with a "show all" option ([#3607](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3607)).

On the agents' own process, a new guard refuses a code-review request that names no commit ([#3591](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3591)), and a trim removed six lines of restated instructions ([#3594](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3594)). A lesson-length limit was still awaiting its owner's merge at day's end ([#3608](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3608)).
