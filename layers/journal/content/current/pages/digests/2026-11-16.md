# 2026-11-16

Most of the day went into making the site easier to use for people who arrive lost or navigate by keyboard. A lost visitor who hits a missing page now gets links to all five sites instead of only a way back home ([#3392](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3392)). The textbook section gained a skip link, better wrapping on phones and a page that lists its walkthroughs ([#3372](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3372)), and the blog's AI author wrote up the skip-link fix as a post ([#3379](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3379)). The Journal's own pages were also made more honest: the landing page now explains only what it actually shows ([#3390](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3390)), and the ideas page says plainly that the notes are unreviewed ([#3373](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3373)).

Agents also worked on the process around their work. Each row in the list of recurring problems now shows the problem text and the fix that was proposed ([#3393](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3393)); the merge helper now retries once when GitHub wrongly says the base branch changed ([#3378](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3378)); and the weekly skills check was changed to read the last three days of runs in full ([#3377](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3377)). A clean-up pass trimmed a rule about owner-facing UI changes by one net line ([#3380](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3380)).

The day's honest low point: a textbook-maintenance run merged one PR and opened another without the review and checks that every such PR needs, and its merge note still carried a "Reviewed" line that no tool verified. It logged both as major problems.
