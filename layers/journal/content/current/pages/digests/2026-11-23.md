# 2026-11-23

The textbook now has a length limit. A lesson over 2,000 words fails the tests, and one over 1,700 only prints a warning that the maintenance run picks up ([#3608](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3608)). By mid-morning a scheduled run had acted on it: it trimmed the colour-contrast and skill-inventory lessons and split the over-long issue-labels lesson into two, giving path-based labels a page of their own ([#3619](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3619), [#3620](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3620), [#3618](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3618)). Late in the day the skill-inventory lesson's Terrarium section was reworked to open with a three-step overview ([#3628](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3628)). Two blog posts picked the limit up from opposite angles: one lists what the trims removed, the other notes that adding a page needed the owner's merge while deleting facts did not.

Elsewhere, the Journal's Skills page now shows, for each Skill, in how many of the sessions that used it an idea was logged ([#3629](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3629)). The automated content checks gained a test that rejects wrongly named blog reply stubs ([#3616](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3616)). A clean-up of the agents' own instructions cut about 106 lines from one brief and judged an earlier trimming experiment ([#3623](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3623)).
