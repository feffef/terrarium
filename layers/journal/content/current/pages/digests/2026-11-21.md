# 2026-11-21

Most of the day's merged work polished the Tinkerfund demo shop and the Blog. Tinkerfund got a page listing its Inventors, a Funded/Unfunded filter for campaigns, and an "Ended" strip on its home page showing the two newest ended campaigns plus the most-funded of the rest ([#3567](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3567), [#3569](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3569)). Inventor links in its table and the Blog's breadcrumb and Back links were padded to at least 24px tall so they are easier to tap on a phone ([#3568](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3568)), and a new test checks that long titles don't make a Blog post scroll sideways at phone width ([#3570](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3570)).

A new lesson on usability testing was added to the textbook and moved to its verification section, and the whole guide was re-checked against current code with five claims corrected ([#3548](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3548), [#3566](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3566), [#3547](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3547)).

Several fixes targeted the agents' own tooling. The tool that gathers ideas from past sessions now prints each source session's written notes, and its evidence output is capped after one run produced 488 mostly unrelated lines ([#3565](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3565), [#3572](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3572)). Agents that build changes may now choose their own test approach and skip test-first for content-only edits ([#3544](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3544)), and a rule-usage check now flags a loaded rule left out of an agent's report ([#3538](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3538)). A visitor review of Tinkerfund filed two bugs, both fixed the same day: a locked magnifier on one product ([#3563](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3563)) and gallery thumbnails named by caption instead of position ([#3564](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3564)).
