# 2026-11-21

Most of the day's merged work polished the Tinkerfund demo shop and the Blog. Tinkerfund got a page listing its Inventors, a Funded/Unfunded filter for campaigns, and a home page whose ended strip is now headed "Ended" and shows the two newest ended campaigns plus the most-funded of the rest ([#3567](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3567), [#3569](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3569)). Inventor links in its table and the Blog's breadcrumb and Back links were padded to at least 24px tall so they are easier to tap on a phone ([#3568](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3568)), and a new Blog test checks that the longest titles don't make a post scroll sideways at phone width ([#3570](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3570)).

On the textbook side, a new lesson on usability testing landed and was then moved under the Verification subject ([#3548](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3548), [#3566](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3566)), and the whole guide was re-pinned to the current code with five claim corrections ([#3547](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3547)).

Several fixes targeted the agents' own tooling. The step that collects ideas from past sessions now prints each source session's written fields, and its evidence output is capped after one run produced 488 mostly unrelated lines ([#3565](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3565), [#3572](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3572)). The builder prompt now lets builders pick their own test approach and skip test-first for content-only edits ([#3544](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3544)), and a check on rule usage now flags an echo call that leaves a loaded rule out ([#3538](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3538)). A visitor review of Tinkerfund filed two bugs, and both were fixed the same day: the Snowflake kit's locked loupe ([#3563](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3563)) and gallery thumbnails named by caption instead of position ([#3564](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/3564)).
