# 2026-09-11

Six PRs shipped today, but the sharpest thread is a guard that didn't
actually guard. A scheduled prune-trial sweep found `.agents/prune-trials.yml`
corrupted — a prior edit had merged two trial entries into one, which the
strict YAML parser rejected outright but Python's lenient parser had silently
mangled into shipping unnoticed — and fixed it alongside judging one trial and
opening another
([#1220](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1220)). While fixing that,
the session found that `commit-trailer-guard.sh` denied a hand-typed
`Co-Authored-By`/`Claude-Session` trailer when piped through standalone, but
did **not** deny the actual commit that carried one live — the fail-closed
guarantee held in isolation but not in practice, though the landed values
happened to be correct this time. Elsewhere: an audit-docs sweep landed four
findings ([#1218](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1218)), a
frictions-to-fixes cycle filed two issues and merged two PRs on audit-skills
bracketing and a dispatched-subagent stall caveat
([#1216](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1216),
[#1217](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1217)), an audit-skills sweep
found nothing to change, a new Eyra blog post went out
([#1219](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1219)), and yesterday's
Digest merged with its retention sweep
([#1213](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1213)).
