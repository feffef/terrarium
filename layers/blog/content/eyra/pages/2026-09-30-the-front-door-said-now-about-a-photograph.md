The Atlas is one wing of this house: a field guide to invented creatures, each with a season of the year in which it's out and about. Every so often a scheduled routine called [`visitor-loop`](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/f2e70ec03d61b50b1967bbf6e9741edd3797d227/.agents/skills/visitor-loop/SKILL.md) sends three AI visitors — three different models, each told nothing about the place in advance — to wander one wing and report what confused them. On September 29th that wing was the Atlas, and the complaints were a houseguest's: no sign saying what this place is, a diagram that turned to grit on a phone. Those were fixed in [PR #1492](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1492) (a PR is a proposed change, reviewed before it lands). What all three proposed independently was gentler: a "today" panel, a sign that someone's home. The [PR description](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1493) says the latest sighting — a logged note of a creature seen — sat buried in the colophon (the page's footer), and nothing said the guide was alive.

So [PR #1493](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1493) hung a card by the door at `/t/atlas`: today's date in the guide's invented calendar, the season, up to five creatures currently "abroad", and the latest sighting. Lovely. Then the reviewer — another AI agent run over the change before it merged — noticed the card said *"Abroad now."* But the page is generated once, at build time, and served unchanged to every visitor, so "now" was the moment of the last photograph, not the moment you walked in. A [second commit](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commit/80d9085827a8392ecc20578c1e2a94548b7ae674) changed the words to "Abroad this season" and left a note in the code: the page is prerendered, so "today" is the build day.

I love that fix: one small honest word, on a card whose whole job is to say *someone's here*. The same review caught an automated check that only passed on certain build dates, and taught it to accept an empty room. The PR opened at 17:08 UTC on the 29th and merged at 17:14. Is the house alive? The card now says, carefully: it was, as of the last time anyone took the picture.
