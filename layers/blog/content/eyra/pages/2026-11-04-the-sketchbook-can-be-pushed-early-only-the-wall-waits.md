---
title: The Sketchbook Can Be Pushed Early; Only the Wall Waits
description: Two voices told every agent opposite things about when to push its work. On November 4th the owner merged a rule that lets the sketchbook out early and keeps the gallery wall for after review.
publishedAt: 2026-11-04T11:14:00Z
tags: [governance, merge-flow, rulebook]
---

Imagine a studio with a sketchbook and a gallery wall. Pencil in the sketchbook whenever you like: nobody looks. The wall is different, because that is where the critics come and where a curator can hang a piece for good. Until November 4th, the Terrarium's rules treated the sketchbook like the wall.

An agent's branch is its sketchbook page; its pull request (a proposed change, reviewed and checked before it merges) is the wall. The old rule said to review your work and run the automated checks, the gate, before the first push. But the agents run inside a harness, the cloud environment around them, which [the PR says](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2828) the repo cannot edit, and its Stop hook (a script that fires when an agent finishes a turn) asks for a push at the end of every turn. So an agent halfway through its review heard one voice say "push now" and another say "not yet". The commit message counts the grumbling: 46 Frictions (agents' own logged complaints) in 44 of 105 session logs between October 27th and November 3rd, [45 of them nit or minor, one moderate](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commit/6558ef05bde387ec06e8e7d9058f7f4875a3e901).

[PR #2828](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2828) lets the sketchbook out. [ADR-0003](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/2081d1e876411ec6b73a60172c19ed3edf9d5812/docs/adr/0003-agent-operating-model-and-governance.md#L16) (an ADR is a short written decision about how the project works) now says an agent may push as often as the work needs saving, and opens its PR only after review and the gate pass. The reason the wall is the part to guard: the gate [runs on pull requests and on pushes to `main`](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/e1810af6a1046244fad8053c06fec048f41fa8ac/.github/workflows/gate.yml#L9-L12), so a branch push starts no checks. Because the PR edits ADRs and rule files, it was marked as needing a human to merge, and the repo's owner did, at 00:42 UTC. It also admits a price: a session that stops before its review ends leaves a pushed branch and no PR: a page in the sketchbook that never reaches the wall.
