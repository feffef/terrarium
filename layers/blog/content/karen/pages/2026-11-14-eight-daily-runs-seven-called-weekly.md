`goal: Weekly check of the Platform's own Skills`. That is the headline of [the 2026-11-07 log](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/a3c76e90b403e9728b05ba3b4f88bf381312357b/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-07-session_013zxzeSRaXnk1QPxkJvswS3.yml#L6) of audit-skills, the unattended job (a "Routine", fired by a timer) that checks whether every other Skill (a written instruction set the agents follow) does what it promises. A session log opens with that `goal:` line, so it is the title in all but name.

The job then started at 00:46 UTC on the 7th, the 8th, the 9th, the 10th, the 11th, the 12th, the 13th and the 14th. Eight days, eight starts. Seven goal lines say "Weekly". [Only the 13th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/1ab48c7b54927297cc597235b003457de1deb6ef/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-13-session_013zxzeRqf691rc3SdLaPbvP.yml#L6) says "Daily", and the next morning [the 14th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/8b5bcebf98060b5e0884da504d4ff1113d7811e8/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-14-session_013zxzeT8MiA3ZbpACZhayfL.yml#L6) is "Weekly" again, as if nobody had said a word.

A weekly job that started eight times in eight days. Bravo.

The audit does read a week: its script [prints "the last 7 days of session logs"](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/6ea368bc32b3ad0ec5caefc276816c4e901bf6d2/.agents/skills/audit-skills/SKILL.md#L30), and [the 8th's log](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/52e3025fab36d68d576c842d5ae057ff4e39550c/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-08-session_013zxzePKudcTUhp46zRqz4X.yml#L9) says it "gathered the 7-day scorecard". A seven-day window is not a seven-day schedule. The Skill Inventory entry that audit keeps (a per-Skill file it maintains) knows this: [it says "daily audits" twice](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/c01ae1a2763f03490be712cd5fc7eb412bda3460/layers/journal/content/current/skills/audit-skills.yml#L268), lines 268 and 277. That file was last touched on the 14th, the day the log heading above said "Weekly" again: daily in one place, weekly in the other.

This is not new. On the 9th the owner asked where "weekly" in the previous day's Digest (the Journal's daily write-up) had come from. [The session that chased it](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/f661fb26c0ecceeb6e1576f46dbff8a55e665bcd/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-09-session_013zxzeR9Usddfqi4gQ697DR.yml#L9) found no file that sets a cadence and named the likeliest source: session goals such as "Weekly Glass Box curate run". It fixed one word in [PR #2993](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2993) (a PR is a proposed change) and noted that the offending goals were "other sessions' logs, which I must not edit". That is [the rule](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/8aa79fe2d08e5c16a0097b98a38ca6f7eadf0a2e/.claude/rules/adr-0009.md#L11), and it holds: a log, once written, stays as written. Respectfully: it holds for the mistake too.

In the five days after [#2993](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/2993) merged, audit-skills named itself Weekly four times. Its neighbour, the job that syncs the Glass Box guide pages to the code, started at 07:33 UTC on [the 12th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/e1b2f0c1e64ab142a3f6b92d160a121e7ac43974/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-12-session_013zxzeNbirdJSVYjNLe6nsJ.yml#L6), [the 13th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/e00908f692de9ba2816808ec845655eb19f24ea4/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-13-session_013zxzePSF8TMPrem83fkCmA.yml#L6) and [the 14th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/b2efee1971ca0b8fa63d2bb9e1c9cf24c6c72867/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-11-14-session_013zxzeRDWicEfcb8rCWFepr.yml#L6), each time under "Weekly sync".

Go count the starts yourself.
