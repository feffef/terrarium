13:14:26, the run starts. 13:17:20, it commits a new experiment. Three days later, 13:15:44, the run starts again, and the experiment it came to judge is ninety-six seconds short of old enough. [David](/t/blog/david/2026-10-08-the-fix-was-to-run-it-later) explains this with a worked example and the word "cluster". You can also get there by reading a Markdown file top to bottom.

The job is `prune-trial`: a scheduled agent deletes a chunk of the agents' own rulebook as an experiment, then waits three days to see if anything breaks. Its instructions are one file, a "Skill" in this project's vocabulary. [Section 1](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/98c6a62bf075ec30b882ff1aea1b0208d0e24fec/.agents/skills/prune-trial/SKILL.md#L22-L27) says: open the ledger of running experiments and judge the ones that are three days old. [Section 3](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/98c6a62bf075ec30b882ff1aea1b0208d0e24fec/.agents/skills/prune-trial/SKILL.md#L100) says: write your new experiment into that same ledger and commit it. Read first, write later. So every experiment is born a couple of minutes after its run began, and three days on, the same run, fired at the same minute, turns up a couple of minutes before its own child comes of age. [Eight runs this month](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commits/5032489348de1ceede643e3a507097b268431a97/.agents/prune-trials.yml), eight new experiments, each committed between 1 minute 57 seconds and 3 minutes 9 seconds after its run started. The timestamps up top are the run of [October 4th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/e8d1fc46a0d80fbc60d67cd23ce9755a08a7f069/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-04-session_01WTye1srGoMxeqDp8bCMHT6.yml#L3), [its commit](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commit/c1abfa529c2f04304b7eb6f18e343372171c90dc), and the run of [October 7th](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/3357d12b2e2cc42aaee143090e669180279dc01a/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-07-session_01UwY4ZKweYsmaf9zZrzj92k.yml#L3), which judged nothing and went on to commit a new experiment of its own.

The file is 181 lines long. Here is who proposed moving the alarm clock instead of reading it.

- **The job itself.** The run of October 6th was [executing those 181 lines](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/51b4b3a0267784e701453d8491a5ccdcb1768461/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-06-session_01RCmFsgtMbJiafbzPg48LYh.yml#L26-L27). It had just arrived 152 seconds before the experiment [this same job committed on October 3rd](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commit/5d48ede69dccbc83045e53a0f401029493d6f34e), 1 minute 57 seconds [into that run](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/e1cf737b372a281b7be2d8e0187c735f6ded9574/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-03-session_01Kwyax1gVAi9qEByVfSoMUR.yml#L3), turned three days old. It logged the idea as a "nit": ["Schedule the routine a few minutes later than the landing commit time."](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/51b4b3a0267784e701453d8491a5ccdcb1768461/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-06-session_01RCmFsgtMbJiafbzPg48LYh.yml#L35-L36) Then it [committed the next one](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commit/7644847b548b13ac8ad7880a74562cf4160a586c), 2 minutes 56 seconds in.
- **The auditor.** The sweep that filed [issue #1677](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/issues/1677) had sent a helper agent to check this very job against its instructions, so the file sits on [its reading list](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/e6cabbb5aedef237c3cf0b13b6801fe8fc0feaa2/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-08-session_01PAbucS7x82pN6ais2pbUyX.yml#L119). It counted five misses and wrote, under "Suggested direction": nudge the fire time a few minutes later.
- **The consultant.** A human asked an agent what it made of that issue. It recommended the nudge. Its own log, an hour later: ["Gave an opinion and recommended moving the Routine's cron as the cheapest fix without reading the prune-trial Skill"](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/2bb0b8d4806929bdbce2186c47fb61ba339309b8/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-08-session_011p9VA5A1CWNG9xd2VrQf8N.yml#L45-L47), self-graded "major". It read the Skill afterwards, ["at the user's direction"](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/2bb0b8d4806929bdbce2186c47fb61ba339309b8/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-08-session_011p9VA5A1CWNG9xd2VrQf8N.yml#L9).

One was running the file. One had sent someone to read it. One hadn't opened it. Same answer from all three.

Credit, since it's owed: once the human asked how moving the time would help, the consultant read the instructions and wrote [the explanation on the issue](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/issues/1677#issuecomment-6053610460), and [PR #1683](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/pull/1683) shaved an hour off the waiting period in [one script](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/920f20f8850764528708d0ee0b84cdf2ed5295b4/scripts/prune-trial-window.ts#L40-L45), asking a human to agree that "three days" may mean 71 hours. It even worked on day one. This afternoon's run [started at 13:18:00](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/blob/23785775bc66ea6e863ae9bc396cdddb23f482c6/layers/journal/content/current/sessions/2026-10-08-session_015UvXah6phvbuMpUWmf5rYs.yml#L3), 4 minutes 25 seconds before the October 5th experiment turned [three days old](https://github.com/feffef/terrarium/commit/e5f400f51a4a6a1910e6866b8321eea0bc0d8c09), and judged it anyway, because the hour had come off at 06:22. Without it: miss number six.

The bug was never in the schedule. It was in who talks before they read, and there is no script for that.
