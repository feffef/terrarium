---
title: How Tinkerfund works
description: Campaigns, Rewards, Pledges and when you are charged — the short version.
---

Tinkerfund is where inventors raise the money to make their inventions real.
Every invention on Tinkerfund is original, and every one of them was built to
solve a problem its inventor considered important.

## Campaigns

Each invention runs a **Campaign** with a funding goal and a deadline. A
Campaign is **Upcoming** until it launches, **Live** while it takes Pledges,
and **Ended** once the deadline passes. An Ended Campaign is **Funded** if it
reached its goal, and **Unfunded** if it did not.

## Rewards

You back a Campaign by choosing a **Reward**: a tier with a price, sometimes
with options such as a colour. Some Rewards are limited, and some limit how
many one Backer may take. **Add-ons** can join a Reward, and you can add
**bonus support** to any Pledge if you feel strongly about it.

## Pledges

Everything you choose for one Campaign becomes one **Pledge**. You can change
or cancel a Pledge until the Campaign ends. Shipping is a flat rate for your
**shipping zone**: Domestic, Europe or Rest of world.

## When you are charged

Your payment stays **pending** until the Campaign is Funded. If it ends
Unfunded, you are not charged. Prices are in euros and include everything
there is to include.

## Stretch goals

Some Campaigns set **Stretch goals** above their goal. Reaching one unlocks
something extra for every Backer, usually a second colour.

## Deals

From time to time a **Promotion** takes something off a Campaign's Rewards.
Active Promotions are listed under Deals. A discount code, where one exists,
can be combined with an automatic discount but not with another code.

## This is a demo

Nothing here is real. No money moves, nothing ships, and no real payment
details are ever asked for. Your Cart and Pledges last until you close the
tab, and **Reset demo** puts everything back the way it was.
