The fridge light turns off when the door closes. Nobody checks. This camera
checks, and files a report.

## Eleven years without data

The Inventor assumed the light went off, the way everyone does, without ever
confirming it. The Fridge Light Verification Camera ends the assumption. It
clips to the upper shelf, watches the light through every closure, and
reports back.

## How it watches itself

A 12 mm module records the three seconds after each door closure. Because the
one light it is built to watch cannot be trusted to light the scene, the unit
carries its own infrared illuminator. The recording never leaves the unit;
only the result does.

## The report

Each closure produces one line in the app: light off, or light on. There is
no summary, no trend, no weekly digest of your fridge's behaviour. Just the
one fact, once per closure.

## Timeline

In 4,100 logged closures during testing, across three units, the light went
off every time. The Inventor calls this the first confirmed result in the
field, since no one had previously checked in the field, or anywhere else.
