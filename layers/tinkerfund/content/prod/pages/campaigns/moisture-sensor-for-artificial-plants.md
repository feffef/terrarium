---

Artificial plants need no water, but until now nothing could confirm this on
an hourly basis.

## Confirms, hourly

The probe pushes into the decorative base and measures moisture, light and
temperature. Every hour it reports the plant as thriving. The Inventor has
checked the firmware and confirmed that the probe cannot report anything
else.

## What it will not do

It will not ask you to water the plant. It has no watering mode, no reminder,
and no setting that would add one. The Inventor considers this the probe's
most important specification.

## Dust, the one alert

The only condition the probe flags is dust on its housing, detected by a
small optical sensor. The suggested wipe interval is 30 days. Ignoring the
alert does not affect the moisture reading, since there is no moisture to
read.

## Sixteen plants, one app

The app pairs with up to 16 plants per home and shows each one individually,
by name, as thriving. A plant cannot be marked otherwise from within the app.
