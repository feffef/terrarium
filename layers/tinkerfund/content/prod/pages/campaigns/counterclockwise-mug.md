Most mugs leave the stirring to you. This one stirs itself, steadily, at
forty turns a minute, and always counterclockwise.

## Why one direction

Early prototypes stirred both ways. Testers spent the first minute of every
coffee choosing. The production mug removes the choice, and with it the
selector switch, a spring and two failure modes.

## How it works

A sealed drive in the base turns a magnetic coupling under the floor of the
mug. The stirring vane sits on top of it and can be lifted out for washing.
There is nothing to set up: fill the mug and it begins.

## Timeline

Tooling for the stoneware body is complete. The drive has passed 2,000 hours
of continuous stirring without a change of direction.
