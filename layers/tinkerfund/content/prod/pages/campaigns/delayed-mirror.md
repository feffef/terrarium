A mirror shows you as you are, looking at it. The Delayed Mirror shows you as
you were three seconds ago, which is the one view an ordinary mirror cannot
give you: yourself, not looking.

## The delay

A camera behind the half-silvered glass front records the room continuously
and plays it back after a fixed delay. At the default setting of 3.0 seconds,
what you see is not your reflection but a recording of yourself from a moment
ago, holding whatever expression you had before you noticed the mirror.

## Why three seconds

The delay is adjustable from 1 to 10 seconds in 0.5-second steps. Under one
second, the effect is difficult to notice. Past six, most testers had already
looked away and back more than once, and the image no longer matched what they
remembered doing. Three seconds was long enough to look away and back exactly
once.

## Seeing yourself now

At a low viewing angle, enough of the glass's own reflection remains visible
alongside the delayed image underneath. Users who need to check their reflection
in real time, rather than three seconds late, can still do so; they simply have
to stand off to the side to find it.

## Shipping

The Delayed Mirror ships within the domestic zone and Europe only. The glass
front is 8 mm thick and the unit weighs 24 kg fully assembled; we could not
find a world-zone courier willing to quote a rate for it.
