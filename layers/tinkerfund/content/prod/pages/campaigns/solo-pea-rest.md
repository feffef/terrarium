Every pea deserves a place to rest between courses. Until now there has been
nowhere suitable to put one.

## The rest

The Solo Pea Rest is a porcelain disc, 64 millimetres across, with a single
dimple sized to hold one garden pea of average size. It is glazed, dishwasher
safe and stackable.

## Why one

A rest for several peas was considered and rejected. Peas kept together tend
to roll. A single pea, properly seated, does not.
