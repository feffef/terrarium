Mornings are difficult. The sun rises on schedule regardless. The Pocket
Sundial gives you back a little control.

## Snooze

Press the button on the rim and a hinged cover closes over the dial. For
nine minutes, the sundial shows no time at all. Then a spring opens it again
and the day resumes.

## A proper sundial

Under the cover is a real instrument. The gnomon is set once for your
latitude, and the dial is accurate to about fifteen minutes whenever the sun
is out.
