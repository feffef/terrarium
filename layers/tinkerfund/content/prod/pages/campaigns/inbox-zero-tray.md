Most inbox trays hold paper, and that turned out to be the problem. The Inbox
Zero Tray has a footprint of A4 and a 2 mm rim, and nothing below it. Anything
placed inside rests on top of the tray, not in it, and is visibly outside.

## Zero by construction

The tray is folded from a single sheet of 1.2 mm cold-rolled steel, so the rim
is the only depth there is. There is no floor beneath it for paper to
accumulate against, because there is no floor.

## The team stacking set

Five trays stack directly on top of one another, rim to rim. The set has an
internal depth of zero millimetres at any height, including fully stacked,
which the Inventor considers the more difficult achievement.

## Where we are

Since installing the prototype, the Inventor reports that the tray has been
empty every day. Production trays have passed 500 stacking cycles without the
rim deforming.
