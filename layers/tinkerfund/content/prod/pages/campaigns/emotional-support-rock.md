Rocks have offered emotional support for as long as there have been rocks.
Until now, none of them could be updated.

## What it does

The Emotional Support Rock sits on your desk and supports you. Once a month
it downloads new firmware, which improves the support in ways that are
described in the release notes.

## What it doesn't do

It has no screen, no speaker, no lights and no moving parts. The support is
delivered entirely through its presence, as with a conventional rock.
