The Jet Lag Rehearsal Lamp moves your evenings toward your destination by
one hour a night, for up to two weeks before you leave. By the day of
departure, you are already tired at the right time.

## The premise

In 2022 the Inventor flew from Valencia to Tokyo to restore a tower clock.
She lost three working days to jet lag. The conclusion was not to avoid jet
lag. It was to have it earlier, at home, when it was convenient.

## How it rehearses

Set the dial to the destination city and the lamp does the rest. Each
evening it dims to warm light one hour earlier than the night before for an
eastbound trip, or one hour later for a westbound one. Each morning it
simulates sunrise at the time the sun rises at the destination. Seven
nights cover seven time zones.

## Results

Sixty-four testers rehearsed 212 trips. On arrival they reported the same
fatigue as before, 5.8 on the Karolinska scale against 5.9 without the lamp.
They had, however, been tired for a week, and said they were used to it.
Most said they would rehearse again.

## The journey home

The Return set includes a second lamp for the hotel, so the flight home can
be rehearsed during the trip. The lamp weighs 1.4 kg and fits in a cabin
bag on its side.

## Stretch goals

At €30,000 a second shade colour, slate, was unlocked on both single-lamp Rewards. At
€36,000 the rehearsal was extended to 14 nights, for journeys across more
than seven time zones. At €45,000 each lamp will ship in a padded case.
