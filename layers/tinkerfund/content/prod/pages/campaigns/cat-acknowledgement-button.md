The button is 110 mm across, felt-topped, and placed on the floor near the
cat's usual position. When the cat is spoken to, it may press the button to
confirm that it heard. A mechanical counter on the side, visible from
standing height, advances by one and does not reset.

## Placement

The button is not tethered to any particular sentence. It registers only
that something was heard, not what, and the Inventor considers this a
feature: a cat asked to get off the counter and a cat told dinner is ready
can use the same button to confirm either.

## The counter

The counter is mechanical and cannot be reset from outside its housing. This
was a deliberate choice, made before any testing began, so that a
disappointing count could not later be attributed to a setting.

## Eleven households

The button was placed in eleven households for eight months. In that time
the counter advanced twice: once by a cat, and once, according to a doorbell
camera reviewed afterward, by a dog investigating the felt. The Inventor
recorded both presses as valid and has proposed no change to the design.

## Shipping

The button ships domestic only. No zone beyond it was added, on the grounds
that a longer supply chain was not the constraint here.
