Most feeders dispense on a timer. This one dispenses on an invitation. Each
meal is scheduled on the cat's own calendar, with a reminder fifteen minutes
before, and the cat's attendance is recorded like anyone else's.

## The invitation

The feeder issues a calendar invitation for each meal to a calendar account
held in the cat's name, with a reminder fifteen minutes before the meal is
due. The invitation is sent in the owner's account language, since the cat
has not stated a preference.

## RSVP

A reader in the feeder checks the cat's collar tag at mealtime. Attendance is
recorded as accepted if the cat is present within two minutes of the
scheduled time, and declined if it is not. Portions are metered from 5 to 80
grams per meal regardless of how the invitation was answered.

## The prototype cat

The prototype cat has attended 412 meals, arriving more than two minutes
after the scheduled time at every one, so none are recorded as accepted. The
Inventor attributes this to the inbox, not the cat, and is reviewing the
subject line.

## Shipping

The feeder ships worldwide. The second calendar account is a digital Reward
and ships nowhere, since a calendar account has no address to send it to.
