Two people can share a bed for years and never agree on who is louder. The
Snore Certificate Printer settles it in writing.

## The dispute

The Inventor built the first unit after a disagreement that had gone on long
enough that neither party trusted the other's account of the night. A
calibrated microphone above the bed was the only witness either side was
willing to accept. It has not been asked to take sides since; it only
measures.

## The measurement

The microphone logs sound pressure continuously from the moment the light
goes off, resolving to 0.1 dB. At wake time, the device finds the single
loudest moment of the night, the time it happened, and how long it lasted
before dropping back under the noise floor. Nothing is discarded until the
certificate is printed.

## The certificate

The result prints on a 58 mm thermal strip: peak level, time of peak,
duration, and a signature line in a fixed typeface unique to the device. It
looks like a receipt because it is treated like one. A single roll lasts 120
nights. On request, the device will print a second copy of the same night's
certificate, so both parties in the room can hold one.

## Two printers, one log

The Dispute pair links two units to the same overnight log, so each side
prints from their own bedside device rather than passing one certificate back
and forth. Both copies report the same number. Neither unit rounds in
anyone's favour.

## Shipping

The printer and one roll ship worldwide. Spare rolls ship in the same package
as a full order and are not sold on their own to a new backer, since a roll
needs a printer to read it.
