No two snowflakes are alike. This has been stated with confidence since the
1880s. The Snowflake Comparison Kit makes it possible to check.

## The claim

The Inventor found the claim in eleven textbooks. None of them recorded a
comparison. A statement about every snowflake is a statement about a very
large number of snowflakes, and she considered it reasonable to start with
two.

## The method

Pre-chill the card outdoors for ten minutes. Catch one flake on each of its
two marked fields, examine both through the loupe, and record the date, the
air temperature and the result in the log book: Alike, or Not alike. A pair
takes about 40 seconds. At −5 °C the flakes last about a minute, which leaves
time to write neatly.

## Results so far

Over three winters in Innsbruck the Inventor has compared 2,316 pairs. All
2,316 were Not alike. This is consistent with the claim. It is also
consistent with 2,316 being a small number. The last page of every log book
is reserved for a positive result, with a postal address and a request to
photograph both fields before they melt.

## Observing in company

The Observatory pair is for two observers in the same snowfall. Flakes cannot
be compared between gardens, since neither survives the walk. Each observer
keeps their own log book; results are combined at the end of the season.
