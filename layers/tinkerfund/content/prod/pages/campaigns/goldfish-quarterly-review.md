The camera clips to any tank and tracks the fish by colour, recording laps,
depth, time near the surface and meals attended. Each Monday the owner
receives a two-page report comparing the week against the objectives set at
the start of the quarter, with a trend line and a short written summary.

## The report

The report opens with the week's totals against the standing objectives, a
trend line against the twelve weeks prior, and a short written summary in
plain language. It prints to two pages regardless of how the week went.

## Objectives

Default objectives are 900 laps per day and presence at every scheduled
feeding. Objectives are set once at the start of the quarter and held fixed
until the next one begins, so a slow week is measured against the quarter's
own standard rather than adjusted to flatter it.

## The quarterly review meeting

A review meeting is scheduled automatically on the first Monday of each new
quarter. The fish attends by being in the tank; the report generated that
week serves as the meeting's minutes. No attendance has yet been recorded as
declined.

## The prototype fish

The prototype fish, a three-year-old comet named in the setup wizard and not
by the Inventor, closed the second quarter at 94% of its lap objective and
100% of its feeding objective. The Inventor's own read is that the lap
objective was set too high in the first quarter and has left it unchanged
regardless, on the grounds that changing it now would break the trend line.

## Shipping

The camera and second camera ship worldwide. The weekly report on its own is
a digital Reward and ships nowhere, for a household that already owns a
compatible camera. The printed binder and desk nameplate ship with the next
scheduled delivery; the second-fish tracking licence is a digital Add-on and
ships nowhere.
