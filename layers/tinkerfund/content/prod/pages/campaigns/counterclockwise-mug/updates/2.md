update:

Passing €24,000 unlocked a third colour. Porcelain white is now available on
the One mug and Two mugs Rewards; you can switch an existing Pledge to it
until the Campaign ends.

The next stretch goal is a matching teaspoon. It does not stir. It is for
ceremonial use.
