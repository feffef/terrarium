Keyboards have grown to more than a hundred keys, and most of them are
pressed rarely, if ever. The One-Key Keyboard keeps the one that matters.

## One key

The key is Enter. It confirms dialogs, submits forms, starts new lines and
accepts the terms and conditions. For everything else there is the keyboard
you already own.

## Built to last

With every press landing on the same switch, durability was the main
engineering problem. The switch is rated for 80 million presses, which at a
normal pace is roughly one working lifetime of agreeing to things.

## Where we are

Production samples have passed drop, cable-pull and spill testing. The goal
is close. If it is reached before the deadline, manufacturing begins the
following week.
