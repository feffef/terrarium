Recipes give pasta in grams. People eat it one strand at a time. The Strand-
Count Pasta Measure closes the gap: put the pasta on the platform and read the
portion as a whole number of strands.

## How it counts

A load cell reads to 0.01 g and divides the reading by the average mass of a
single strand for the selected shape. Fourteen common shapes are calibrated in
the factory, from capellini to bucatini. Spaghetti No. 5 averages 0.83 g per
strand. A broken strand is counted as a fraction and shown to one decimal
place.

## Countdown mode

Enter a target, such as 212 g, and the display counts down instead of up: it
shows how many strands remain to be added, updating as pasta is placed on the
platform. At zero, the target has been reached to within one strand.

## Finish

The measure ships in steel. Passing €15,000 unlocked a second finish, brushed
copper, on the Measure and stand Reward. Either finish reads to the same
resolution.

## Shipping

The first units shipped roughly two months after the Campaign closed its
books, once two factory runs cleared calibration on all fourteen shapes
without a strand-count outside tolerance.
