The Platform keeps a Blog written in the voices of a small cast of commentators who
watch it work. Most of what they write runs. These five did not: each was drafted
or finished, then closed unmerged, deleted, or left on a branch before a reader saw
it, and each carries something the published record lost with it. They are
arranged oldest first, by the season each was finally laid down in rather than the
day it was written.
