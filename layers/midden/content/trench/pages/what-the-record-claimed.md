---
title: What the Record Claimed
description: Four finds where the Platform's own record held something that was not so, or no longer was — an entry on the list of recommended skills for a skill that was never there; a page recommended, delivered as a plan, and killed in one word; a stand-in entry for a skill that said outright it did not exist; and a table of rules that stopped matching the project.
---

Three of the four finds here are entries in the Platform's own record that were,
in different ways, not true of the Platform: a recommendation for a skill that did
not exist, a recommendation for a page that was never built, and a stand-in for
a skill that announced its own absence. None of the three is a lie. The first two are the
ordinary way a written record drifts from the thing it describes when nobody
has occasion to check; the third was a fiction from the start, labelled as one,
and outlived the reason it was written. The fourth is a whole table, not an
entry: a snapshot of the project's written rules that was kept in step by hand,
with near-daily edits as checks were removed and documents swept, and that the
owner retired when it no longer matched.
