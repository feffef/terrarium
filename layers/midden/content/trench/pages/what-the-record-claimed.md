---
title: What the Record Claimed
description: Three finds where the Platform's own record held something that was not so — an entry on the list of recommended skills for a skill that was never there, a page recommended, delivered as a plan, and killed in one word, and a stand-in entry for a skill that said outright it did not exist.
---

All three finds here are entries in the Platform's own record that were, in
different ways, not true of the Platform: a recommendation for a skill that did
not exist, a recommendation for a page that was never built, and a stand-in for
a skill that announced its own absence. None is a lie. The first two are the
ordinary way a written record drifts from the thing it describes when nobody
has occasion to check; the third was a fiction from the start, labelled as one,
and outlived the reason it was written.
