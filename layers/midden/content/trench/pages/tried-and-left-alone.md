two were closed on 3 October 2026 and have no other connection: one was an
upgrade that passed every check, the other an experiment that removed the
project's own rules to see what would happen. Three days later a bot proposed
the same upgrade again, with one more beside it, and was told to stop. In every
case the project went on as before.
