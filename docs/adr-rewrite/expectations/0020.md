## Summary by part of the Platform

**Code and tests.** These need a mechanical rule. `scripts/trust.ts` holds the two `authorAssociation` sets. `merge-pr.ts` refuses fork and Public-authored PRs and ignores verdict markers from Public authors. `poll-guest-tickets.ts` screens guests by the same split. The label workflow applies `trusted` and uses a plain `pull_request` trigger. All of them name ADR-0020 as the source of the split. They need it to keep "write access = Trusted, detected by `authorAssociation`" and "Public PRs are human-merged". The three catalog files cite it for "write isolation", which is a miscite.

**Skills.** clear-backlog, clear-backlog-explore, guest-build and guest-intake rely on rules 2 and 3 by number. A Public issue is never built or labelled `ready-for-agent` without a Trusted yes. A PR built from a Public issue waits for a human merge even where `merge-pr.ts` would accept it. An unmarked OWNER comment is a Trusted override.

**Agent docs and CLAUDE.md.** These are guest-contributions, issue-tracker, pr-workflow, triage-labels and the overview map. They use ADR-0020 as the home of the tiers. Public is decided by `authorAssociation`, not by a label. The code-execution boundary is the human-only merge. The fork-PR Actions approval setting must be "all outside contributors". `ready-for-agent` is gated by requester trust. SECURITY.md names ADR-0020 as the trust-tier line.

**Other ADRs and rules.** ADR-0023 is the heaviest caller (13 refs). It quotes rules 1, 2 and 3 by number, restates the Why (prompt injection, gate blind to intent, merge = RCE) and cites a shared-credential limit. It also says ADR-0020 holds the exact enum values. ADR-0003 and ADR-0004 carry dated "Amended by ADR-0020 (2026-07-11)" notes that link the file name. ADR-0004 calls ADR-0020 the single home of the requester-trust axis, including the security-review and enforcement mechanics. ADR-0022 cites it for "Trusted" and for the rule that Public issues are never self-green-lit. ADR-0025 miscites it.

**Glossaries (`CONTEXT.md`).** The Trusted and Public entries point to ADR-0020 for why the line sits at write access, not at the owner, and for the mechanical detection. They also restate the Public rules: untrusted data, no implementation without a Trusted green-light, never auto-merged.
