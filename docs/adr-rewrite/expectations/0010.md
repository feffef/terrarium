## Checklist (most relied-on first)

1. **Where do digests live?** Claim: a digest is an ordinary page in the
   Journal's `pages` collection, under `/digests/<YYYY-MM-DD>`, and it has no
   collection of its own (a separate one was put off until a second need).
   *Mechanic*, held in `scripts/digest.ts` (`DIGESTS_DIR`) and read by
   `layers/journal/app/utils/dashboard.ts`. 6 refs: 5 explains, 1 enforces.
   E.g. dashboard.ts:249, digest.ts:31, domain.md:53.
2. **Which days get a digest?** Claim: one digest per finished UTC day. Today
   never gets one. Any finished day that had activity but has no digest is
   filled in, oldest first. *Mechanic*, held in `scripts/digest.ts`
   (`dayIsClosed`, `closedUndigestedDays`) and checked by
   `tests/unit/digest.spec.ts`. 5 refs: 3 enforces, 1 explains, 1 repeats.
   E.g. digest.ts:87, digest.ts:155.
3. **How are digests shown on the Journal home page?** **Conflict.** Side A:
   the page reads digest pages live, so a new digest shows up without a build
   step, and each digest opens in place like a session card (index.vue:44,
   digest.ts:21, dashboard.ts:249). Side B: the `digest` Skill writes each
   day's headline into the index's "recent digests" preview
   (tenant.config.ts:104). *Decision*. 4 refs: 3 explains, 1 points.
4. **What goes into a digest, and who writes it?** Claim: it summarizes
   activity across all Tenants and is built from existing records (git
   history, merged PRs, commits, session logs and their frictions). Nobody
   writes it from scratch. *Decision*. 3 refs: 2 explains, 1 repeats.
   E.g. CONTEXT.md:31, digest.ts:1.
5. **How is the work split between the script and the Skill?** Claim:
   `scripts/digest.ts` lists the missing days and gathers one day's material
   as JSON. The `digest` Skill turns that into prose. *Mechanic*, held in
   `scripts/digest.ts`. 2 refs: 1 explains, 1 enforces. E.g. digest.ts:1,
   digest.spec.ts:1.
6. **Is a digest ever changed after it is written?** Claim: no. Once its day
   ends, a digest is never rewritten. *Decision*. 1 ref (repeats).
   CONTEXT.md:31. See item 7.
7. **May old digests be moved out of the live Space?** Claim: yes. A cleanup
   script moves old digests from `current` to `archived`, keeps a fixed
   number in `current`, and the digest helper checks both places. **Possible
   conflict** with item 6, because moving a page changes its Space and URL.
   The ADR should say whether a move counts as a change. *Decision*, done by
   `scripts/archive-journal-content.ts`. 3 refs: 1 explains, 1 enforces,
   1 points. E.g. archive-journal-content.ts:4, digest.ts:31,
   terrarium-overview.md:865.
8. **Does a digest have its own page URL?** **Conflict.** Side A: yes, each
   digest has a standalone `digests/<day>` page served by the Journal's
   catch-all route ([...slug].vue:5). Side B: no, a digest has no page of its
   own and is opened by a `#fragment` on the Journal home page
   (timeline.ts:32). *Decision*. 2 refs, both explains.
9. **Can another Tenant use `/digests/<date>`?** Claim: the `/digests/` path
   means a digest only in the Journal. A page at that path in another Tenant
   is an ordinary page, and the Commons Timeline only treats `journal` pages
   as digests. *Decision*, enforced in `layers/commons/app/composables/timeline.ts`.
   2 refs, both explains. E.g. timeline.ts:32, adr/0025:152.
10. **What is a digest's one-line headline?** Claim: it is the page's
    `summary` field, which falls back to `description` and then to an empty
    string. *Mechanic*, held in `layers/journal/tenant.config.ts` and
    `layers/journal/app/utils/dashboard.ts`. 2 refs, both explains.
    E.g. tenant.config.ts:104, dashboard.ts:249.
11. **Where do digests sit within the Journal?** Claim: digests are one of
    the Journal's kinds of content, next to inventories (rewritten in place)
    and session logs (written by hand and only added to). *Decision*.
    2 refs: 1 points, 1 repeats. E.g. adr-0008.md:12, CONTEXT.md:31.
