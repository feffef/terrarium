  ADR-0028 amendment)". Breaks if the bound is folded into the body.
- `docs/research/content-client-queries-without-wasm.md:34`: "ADR-0028 is the

- `docs/adr/0019-...md:199` is itself a dated amendment of ADR-0019 that

### 2. Conflicts

- **Always vs mostly.** Test names (`smoke.spec.ts:60`, `blog.spec.ts:126`)
  and `nuxt.config.ts:16` state navigation reads the payload without the
  client DB; `adr-0019` rule:15 and ADR-0019:199 say "most"/"mostly", with
  exceptions. Compatible if the ADR names the exceptions (item 2); a rewrite
  must keep them.
- **Two homes for the config.** `research/...md:34` makes ADR-0028 the home
  of the final configuration; `research/...md:163` names "ADR-0028 and the two
  `nuxt.config.ts` files" as source of truth. One-home conflict.

### 3. Misplaced expectations (ADR-0030)

- Final configuration values (`payloadExtraction: 'client'`, `/t/**`
  `maxAge: 60, swr: false`, `lru-cache` `max: 200`) expected to live in the
  ADR (`research/...md:34,163,208`). Constants belong in `nuxt.config.ts`.
- Byte measurements (6-144 KB vs ~0.5-1.85 MB) expected in the ADR
  (`research/...md:34`). Measurements belong in the research note.
- Tinkerfund's `varies: ['accept-language']` (item 5): if the ADR lists
  per-Tenant vary keys, that is a list that belongs in each Tenant's config.
