## Summary by part of the Platform

**Code and tests.** Need ADR-0015 as the authority for two mechanisms: the
pack-Skill integrity pin (`installedSha256`, `verify:skills-lock`, its Gate
step) and the Inventory schema (`role` limits, `observations` log with 40-day
retention). They cite specific dated amendments (2026-07-13, 2026-09-27) and an
undated "amendment" for the pin, so the rewrite must keep those decisions
findable. `audit-skills.ts` and its tests rely on the "helper gathers, session
judges" split.

**Skills.** `audit-skills` needs its write scope (Inventory `.yml` + issues;
Skill-text changes go to a human). `frictions-to-fixes` needs the rule that a
pack Skill's `SKILL.md` is off limits and that repo-specific notes go to its
Inventory entry instead.

**Other ADRs and rules.** ADR-0005 (and its rule) and ADR-0027 rely on "pack
Skills stay as installed, the Gate rejects edits". ADR-0003 relies on an
"evidence rule" for `audit-skills`' merge scope and on the remit idea
(`sync` = `digest` + `audit-skills`); ADR-0010 on `audit-skills` being
scheduled. ADR-0005 also assumes ADR-0015 reflects the `.agents/skills/` layout.

**Agent docs.** Only the overview diagram, which maps the `skills` and
`inventory` nodes to ADR-0015 as their reason.
