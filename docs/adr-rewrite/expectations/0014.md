## Summary by part of the Platform

**Code and tests.** `modules/routing.ts`, `tsconfig.node.json`,
`vitest.config.ts` and the e2e/unit helpers implement the decision and cite it
as their reason. They need the ADR to say the map is derived from
`expand(loadManifests())` at prepare/build time as `#routing`, that nothing is
committed, and to keep a numbered wiring list (`routing.ts:49` names "wiring #3",
the `tsconfig.node.json` mapping). The tests also lean on it for "the entry-route
list comes from the same expansion". Four Tenant manifests and `shared/manifest.ts`
cite it only for "no generator, no codegen step".

**Tenant content (Midden).** The Midden's artifacts and dig-season comments use
ADR-0014 as the historical event that removed the committed routing map, the
generator and the drift gate. They need the ADR to keep saying that, with its
date (2026-07-05) intact; they quote no current ADR wording.

**Other ADRs and rules.** ADR-0006, 0007 and 0013 carry status lines and
amendment blocks saying ADR-0014 amends or supersedes them, linking to
`0014-build-time-virtual-routing-module.md`. The ADR must record those
relationships and keep its filename. ADR-0016 and 0025 rely on its scope: the
map is Space-scoped and `pages`-only. ADR-0025 names "the ADR-0014 pattern" as a
reusable build-time projection, so the ADR must state that shape clearly. ADR-0004
points to it for "the generator is gone" and for the manifest-derived entry-route
sweep. The ADR-0002 rule cites it for "routes are expanded at build time".

No Skills, agent docs, CLAUDE.md or `CONTEXT.md` glossaries reference ADR-0014.
