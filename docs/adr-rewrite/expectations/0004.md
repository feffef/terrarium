## By part of the Platform

**Code and tests.** Code needs three things. (1) A clear Human-only boundary
that is mechanically enforceable: `merge-pr.ts` and `clear-backlog-build.js`
read the rule file's `paths`, and the merge-pr specs pin that `docs/adr/**` is on it.
(2) The L0 to L3 layer names, with L3 defined as unique, correctly scoped
collection keys, checked at both expansion (`expand.ts`, `expand.spec.ts`) and
resolution (`routing.ts`, `routing.spec.ts`). (3) A statement that
presentation-only layer code stays outside the isolation core. About 24 Tenant
pages and configs justify themselves that way. The gate scripts
(`gate.ts`, `action.yml`, e2e support) cite the 2026-10-12 amendment for the
Skill-file skip and the one-build e2e rule. The guards and their specs cite the
2026-07-30 amendment for "unattended hook ⇒ Human-only" and the pure-core and
`--dry-run` reviewability bar. Three files cite "a new dependency escalates" as
the reason they hand-stub the DOM instead of adding one.

**Skills.** `audit-docs`, `frictions-to-fixes`, `guest-build`, `guest-intake`,
`prune-trial` and the `clear-backlog` workflow need the ADR to name the
human-merge exceptions and to point to the rule's `paths` as the safe-surface
test. `frictions-to-fixes` also relies on the 2026-07-30 amendment's worked
examples for "changes untested/untestable runtime behaviour" and on "gate =
build / validate / isolation / smoke". The guest Skills need "adds a dependency"
and "touches a Human-only surface" as escalation triggers.

**Agent docs and CLAUDE.md.** CLAUDE.md and `pr-workflow.md` rely on one
sentence: agents self-merge on green, and the ADR-0004 rule names the
exceptions. They also point to the rule file for paths and use `gate:scoped`
before a push. `guards.md` needs "guards are Human-only to merge, because unit
tests can't observe live interception". `tenant-layers.md` needs "a new
dependency is Platform-wide and escalates". README and SECURITY name ADR-0004
as the safety gate and L3 as the isolation guarantee.

**Other ADRs and rules.** These are the heaviest callers. ADR-0003, ADR-0020,
ADR-0022 and ADR-0023 lean on ADR-0004 as the merge boundary and on the
exceptions list (Public/guest, dependency, Human-only surfaces). ADR-0020
quotes "low-risk auto-merge tier", "blast-radius tiers" and the
"untested-behaviour blind spot". ADRs 0006, 0007, 0010, 0013, 0014, 0016, 0018
and 0025 rely on "isolation-critical surfaces are Human-only, presentation is
not". ADR-0026 relies on "high-risk set", on the 2026-07-11 amendment (no
`workflow` scope; drift), and on "Human-only was always a merge rule, not an
edit rule". ADR-0019 and ADR-0024 rely on "escalation axes" beyond the file
list. ADR-0005 cites the 2026-10-12 amendment. ADR-0029 needs the
`adr-0004-human-merge.md` topic-rule convention.

**Glossaries.** `CONTEXT.md` defines **Gate** (objective, layered, Floor/Heavy/Full,
CI authoritative) and **Human-only** (the rule's `paths`; merge-only) against
ADR-0004. **Trusted** says any Trusted human merges the exceptions, and
**Friction** says that a CI failure after a passing `gate:scoped` is `major`.
