**C1. Which PRs may an agent merge itself?** (question) — *Summary*
Agents merge their own PRs on a green gate (the 2026-10-10 amendment); ADR-0004
names the exceptions. 11 refs: quotes 7, explains 3, repeats 1. E.g.
`docs/adr/0004-objective-safety-gate.md:36`, `docs/agents/pr-workflow.md:88`.
**Conflict:** `0004:154` (2026-07-19) says an ordinary work PR is always
human-merged; `0009:30`, `0009:47` quote a "no self-merge" rule; `0020:50` says
the "no self-merge" rule is unchanged.

(`auto-triage:69`, `0022:22`); a standing green-light when a Trusted user starts
`auto-triage` (`0022:51`); never a Public issue (`0020:95`). 11 refs: explains 10,
repeats 1. E.g. `docs/adr/0027-prune-trials.md:6`, `.agents/skills/auto-triage/SKILL.md:69`.

## Summary by part of the Platform

**Code and tests.** Only two live expectations. `shared/schemas/session.ts:37`
relies on "open a gated PR at closure, merge later" to justify `in-review`.
`.github/dependabot.yml:2` relies on a `dependabot` ledger row that auto-merges
minor/patch bumps. The 13 test hits are fixtures that use `adr-0003.md` as the
sample always-loaded rule. Nothing enforces ADR-0003 directly.

**Skills.** These need the auto-merge ledger rows to exist, keep their names, and
state each Skill's bounded scope (`digest`, `audit-docs`, `blog-post`,
`prune-trial`, `visitor-loop`, `frictions-to-fixes`). They also need the
"2026-10-10 amendment" (self-merge on green) to stay findable, the review-agent role
(review and merge, never author; escalate high-risk), "ready-for-agent is the
green-light", and the closure trigger "opening a PR".

**Agent docs.** `pr-workflow.md` cites ADR-0003 as the reason tiers merge on green,
as the reason pushing opens the PR, and as the home of the ledger. It also
restates the ledger in "Per-tier merge authority". The overview diagram names
ADR-0003 as the reason for routines, sessions, PRs and the maintainer, and
restates per-routine merge scopes. `SECURITY.md` names ADR-0003 as half of the
agent-governance boundary.

**Other ADRs and rules.** These lean on ADR-0003 most. They need: the
gated-PR default that ADR-0009/0010/0012/0015 carve against; the
propose-vs-implement split and Trusted green-light (ADR-0015/0020/0022, and the
"Green-lit by a human (ADR-0003)" headers in ADR-0024/0026/0027/0028/0029); the
enumerated remits `sync`/`consolidate`/`triage`/`codify` that ADR-0022 extends and
ADR-0007/0008/0010/0015 cite; the auto-merge exemption ledger (ADR-0004/0010/0015/0027);
the review-agent (ADR-0004); the autonomy-tier axis (ADR-0020); and the rule's
session-start status (ADR-0029). Several quote dated amendments and old wording.

**Glossary (`CONTEXT.md`).** The Trusted entry needs ADR-0003 to define the
net-new green-light that a Trusted user gives, and it relies on agents merging
their own PRs.
