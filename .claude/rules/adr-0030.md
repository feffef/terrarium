# ADR-0030: ADRs document a decision, not its history

An ADR states one decision as it stands today: its scope, why, and the
rejected alternatives. To change it, rewrite it in place, with no amendments,
dates or history notes, and in the same PR rewrite its summary rule and
repoint its callers. Numbers, procedures and commands live in code, a Skill or
`docs/agents/`, never in the ADR. A full rewrite follows `docs/adr/TEMPLATE.md`.

Read `docs/adr/0030-adrs-document-a-decision-not-its-history.md` (~1100 tokens) to change it or review work it governs, or to see the template and what counts as history.
