# ADR-0009: A session's own log goes straight to `main`, without a PR

Invoke `close-session` when you open a PR, and again when the work merges,
stops or is blocked; re-invoking it is safe and replaces your earlier log. You
write the judgement half and record every Friction honestly; the hooks derive
the rest, and `scripts/session/log-session.ts` commits that one file straight to
`main`. Keep logs out of your branch and PRs (only an outside agent's log
travels in its PR), and never edit another session's log. Add a field as optional, or add a new schema version beside the old one.

Read `docs/adr/0009-session-logs-commit-directly-to-main.md` (~1300 tokens) to change it or review work it governs, or to see which log fields you write and how the schema may grow.
