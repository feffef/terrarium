# ADR-0021: Platform and Tenant domain contexts

Domain vocabulary is split into contexts, indexed by `CONTEXT-MAP.md`. Root
`CONTEXT.md` is the Platform context: terms every agent needs whatever the
task, plus a `## Tenants` roster. A Tenant's own terms and its purpose go in
`layers/<tenant>/CONTEXT.md`. ADRs record Platform architecture only, so a
demo Tenant gets no ADR. A new Tenant adds its `CONTEXT.md` and a roster line.
