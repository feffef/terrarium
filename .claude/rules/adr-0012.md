# ADR-0012: Pingbacks are written at authoring time

Cross-Persona links are copied in at write time, so every Blog page read stays
inside one Space. When a Persona reacts to another's post, the `blog-post`
Skill adds, in one gated PR, the reaction to its own `pages` and a Pingback to
the target Persona's `pingbacks`, with the reacting post's persona, path,
title and blurb inlined. Renaming or deleting a reaction means updating its
Pingback too.
