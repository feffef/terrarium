# ADR-0016: A Tenant's own layer may hold pages at its root, `/t/<tenant>`

Put a Tenant's root page in its own layer, at
`layers/<tenant>/app/pages/t/<tenant>/index.vue`, never in a Platform page
that serves every Tenant. It may show a landing page or redirect to one Space.
Read its own Tenant's Spaces only through `resolveSpaceRoute`, and other
Tenants only through the catalog (ADR-0025). A hand-built static page,
`layers/<tenant>/app/pages/t/<tenant>/<name>.vue`, may sit beside it: it holds
its own text, reads no Space data, and its name is never a Space slug of that
Tenant. Keep these pages out of the manifest and the routing map. The smoke
test never visits them, so test them in the Tenant's own tests.

Read `docs/adr/0016-tenant-root-layer-routes.md` (~1000 tokens) to change it or review work it governs, or to see why a landing page isn't a Space.
