# ADR-0016: Tenant root pages

A Tenant's layer may own a page at its root, `/t/<tenant>`, in
`layers/<tenant>/app/pages/t/<tenant>/index.vue`. It is layer presentation,
not routing: it stays out of the manifest, the routing map and the smoke
spec's entry routes, and reads Space data only through `resolveSpaceRoute`.
Put root pages in Tenant layers, and test each one in its own Tenant.
