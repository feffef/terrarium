# ADR-0016: A Tenant's own layer may hold a page at its root, `/t/<tenant>`

Put a Tenant's root page in its own layer, at
`layers/<tenant>/app/pages/t/<tenant>/index.vue`, never in a Platform page
that serves every Tenant. It may show a landing page or redirect to one Space.
Read its own Tenant's Spaces only through `resolveSpaceRoute`, and other
Tenants only through the catalog (ADR-0025); keep root pages out of the
manifest and the routing map. The smoke
test never visits a root page, so test it in the Tenant's own tests.

Read the full ADR (~800 tokens) before you change this decision, or when you design what a Tenant shows above its Spaces:
docs/adr/0016-tenant-root-layer-routes.md
