# ADR-0008: The Journal is the Platform's logbook, not a status report

The Journal (`journal`) is Platform infrastructure, not a demo Tenant. Its
session logs are the source of truth: keep them and never regenerate them from
repo state. Inventories (the Skill Inventory, the Friction Ledger) are derived
and refreshed in place; Digests are derived daily (ADR-0010).

Read `docs/adr/0008-self-documentation-tenant-is-a-journal.md` (~600 tokens) before you change this decision, or when you decide what the Journal keeps or refreshes.
