# ADR-0027: Test an instruction by removing it

The `prune-trial` Skill cuts one problem's instructions to their goal, or
rewrites one Skill or doc plainly, and lets later sessions' Frictions judge
the cut. ADRs and their summary rules change in a trial only when a Trusted
user starts it; a human merges it, and a bad verdict restores the cut text.
If an open trial
(`.agents/prune-trials.yml`) covers text you would change, don't restore or
reword it: log a Friction and work around it. Repointing a reference that an
ADR change requires is allowed. When one of our instructions
causes a problem, cut or simplify it before adding one.

Read `docs/adr/0027-prune-trials.md` (~1000 tokens) to change it or review work it governs, or to see what a trial may change and how its verdict is judged.
