# ADR-0027: Prune trials

Rules are tested by removing them. Each `prune-trial` run cuts one problem's
prose to its goal, or rewrites a Skill or doc plainly while keeping what it
decided, and merges on a green gate. ADRs and their summary rules are out of
scope. Trials in `.agents/prune-trials.yml`
stand about three days from landing; a `major` or `blocker` Friction they caused
restores one line. If you hit a problem inside a trial's territory, log a
Friction and continue.

If this touches your task, read the full ADR first:
docs/adr/0027-prune-trials.md
