# ADR-0024: Mermaid diagrams ship as pre-rendered, committed SVG

Mermaid diagrams are drawn into committed SVGs in `app/assets/mermaid/` when
they are written; the browser loads no mermaid code, and the app must never
need a browser. After adding or editing a diagram, run `pnpm render:mermaid`
(needs Chromium) and commit every SVG change; the gate fails otherwise. Colours
follow the Tenant through `--diagram-*` variables; font and layout are fixed in
the SVG. Each Tenant draws in one font: the default, unless its layer's
`mermaid-font.ts` names another, whose family must be the `@font-face` its
pages serve for that woff2.

Read `docs/adr/0024-build-time-mermaid-prerender.md` (~1000 tokens) to change it or review work it governs, or to see what the gate checks in an SVG.
