# ADR-0015: Pack Skills stay as installed; every Skill is graded by need

Never edit a Skill listed in `skills-lock.json`; `pnpm verify:skills-lock`
fails the gate on it. Put notes on a pack Skill, and problems with its text,
in its Skill Inventory entry, never in an issue. `importance` grades how much a
Skill matters when its kind of work comes up, not how often it runs
(`audit-skills` defines the grades), and moves only on cited session evidence. Keep `role` free of
PR, issue and session ids; they go in `observations`. `audit-skills` edits
only Inventory entries, Routine run costs and issues, never a Skill.

Read `docs/adr/0015-audit-skills-and-conditional-importance.md` (~1000 tokens) to change it or review work it governs, or to see when a grade may move and when a finding becomes an issue.
