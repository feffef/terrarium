# ADR-0015: Skill Inventory and audit-skills

Skill Inventory `importance` grades how essential a Skill is when its kind of
work happens, not how often it runs (grades in `CONTEXT.md`). A grade changes
only with 2 or more cited sessions. `audit-skills` checks that our own Skills
fire and deliver, and keeps the Inventory in line. Pack Skills stay as
installed (`pnpm verify:skills-lock`); repo advice for one goes in its
Inventory entry.

If this touches your task, read the full ADR first:
docs/adr/0015-audit-skills-and-conditional-importance.md
