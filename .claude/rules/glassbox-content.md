# Glass Box: keep facts that change out of the prose

A Glass Box page is pinned to its `revision` but read long after it. Its prose,
and every diagram label, never holds a count that changes, when something runs,
a PR, issue, session or commit id, a line number, or a full list that grows:
link the live list instead. Only a Walkthrough's *A real case* may cite them.
