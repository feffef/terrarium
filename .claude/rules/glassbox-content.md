# Glass Box content

Pages follow `.agents/skills/glassbox-lesson/writing-pages.md`. The owner's hard
rules for them are in `.agents/skills/glassbox-lesson/owner-decisions.md`: never
go against one; if one seriously hurts your task log a Friction, and if you have
a good content proposal that clashes with one log an idea. The workshop notes,
`layers/glassbox/content/workshop/pages/index.md`, hold the guide's weak spots,
the owner's direction and the agents' soft decisions, which evidence may
overrule.

**Keep facts that change out of the prose.** A Glass Box page is pinned to its
`revision` but read long after it. Its prose, and every diagram label, never
holds a count that changes, when something runs, a PR, issue, session or commit
id, a line number, or a full list that grows: link the live list instead. Only
a Walkthrough's *A real case* may cite them.
The `workshop` Space is state, not prose to read later: it may hold all of these.
Two lists are exceptions, kept complete: *Scheduled tasks* lists every Routine,
and *Self-improvement loop* every Skill in the loop.
