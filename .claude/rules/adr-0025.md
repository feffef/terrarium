# ADR-0025: Cross-Tenant reads through collection kinds

Cross-Tenant reads are opt-in per Collection. A Collection opts in by naming a
kind from `shared/kinds.ts`; its schema is that kind's contract merged with
its own fields. `modules/catalog.ts` derives `#catalog`, and Aggregators (the
Commons Tenant) read only through `queryAcrossTenants`
(`app/composables/catalog.ts`), keeping their adapters in their own layer.

If this touches your task, read the full ADR first:
docs/adr/0025-cross-tenant-catalog-and-collection-kinds.md
